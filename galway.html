Galway

On Weekend 2 we went to Galway! Here is some of my favorite parts of the trip!

Computer Museum

Our only cultural event during this weekend was the Computer Museum and it didn't disappoint. We learned all about old technology!

They had a phone in there, and if you dialed 22 you could talk to someone across the room!

Vintage Shopping

While we were waiting to check into the hostel, we went shopping at a vintage market! There was some interesting peices.

Pasta Factory

This was the best pasta ever!! I got a truffle sauce!

Walking & Talking

We had a lot of bonding time and did a lot of walking around and looking for something fun to do. We walked a couple miles to an old lighthouse.

We saw what looked like a shipwreck, but on our walk back it was back in the water!

Colored Houses

There was so many cool sights to see like these cute colorful houses. I would love to live in one of these!

Shopping

The cutest little street was full of resturants, pubs, and shops. We saw so many shops with 100% wool sweaters.

Galway is the home of the Claddagh Ring! I bought one which I've been wanting ever since applying for the program.

Xian Street Food

We got hungry after shopping and wanted something other than pub-food! We got chinese food at Xian. It was super good and filling!

The pitchers of water were actually super big here, which is rare for Ireland I've learned. We were not thirsty after!

Breakfast

While killing time before our bus to the Cliffs of Moher, we had breakfast in a hotel lobby. It was super super yummy!
