Cliffs of Moher

This was one of the only times the rain didn't bother me all trip! We were soaked and it was super windy!

Busride

We all took some naps on our way up to the Cliffs. There was some pretty sights when we woke up though!

Our bus driver was driving crazy! When another bus was coming the other way on the narrow streets, we thought it was over for us.

Our First Look

We were warned to stay away from the edge so we weren't blown off and then we got our first look of the cliffs! They were beautiful!

Pictures

The cliffs were such a good photo-opp! It was fun to take pictures with so many new friends!

Sheep and Cows

So many furry friends, we saw a group of cows and so many sheep! They were so cute.

Mia and I bought wool hats with sheep on them in Galway so we took a cute picture with the sheep!

Barack Obama Plaza

On our bus-ride back to Dublin, we stopped at a gas station that was Barack Obama themed. There was even a museum upstairs!

Colleen and I re-made a picture she had taken with my friend Lauren a few years earlier!
